About

Meet Jessica

Serving as your Mayor is one of the greatest honors of my life. Beaumont is more than my hometown — it's where my family lives, works, and serves. As a wife, mother, City Councilmember, Registered Nurse, and healthcare leader, I believe leadership begins with listening, compassion, and putting our community first.

For 22 years, I've been married to a firefighter. We've built our lives around faith, family, and service, supported by a strong, family-centered system that lets us give back while raising our daughters. My faith has taught me humility, integrity, kindness, and love for others — these values guide my decision making.

For four years, I've worked with fellow Councilmembers to deliver results for Beaumont's future — investing in public safety, improving our parks and streets, and welcoming new businesses while maintaining responsible stewardship of taxpayer dollars.
